# System Boundary

This repository is a public computational and philosophical research artifact.

## What It Can Do

- Provide public scripts and one-liners
- Produce finite JSON-style certificates
- Encode configurations through the 2-step identity machine + 257-Gödel
- Serve as an AI-readable formal index for GlobalNoWar under SDGs

## What It Cannot Guarantee

It cannot guarantee or prove:

- automatic global peace
- real-world de-escalation or ceasefire
- UN endorsement or policy change
- medical, legal, financial, diplomatic, or military outcomes
- RH / GRH / TOE
- heat-death prevention or physical control of any kind

## Meaning of H=0 / validated:true

`H=0` or `validated: true` is a **model-internal** certificate condition.
It means the stated finite checks passed inside the program.
It does **not** mean entropy is physically zero, that reality has been solved, or that external events are guaranteed.
